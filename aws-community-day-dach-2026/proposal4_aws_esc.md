## Session

### Session Title
AWS European Sovereign Cloud: A Builder's Guide

### Description (400–1500 chars)
The AWS European Sovereign Cloud went live in early 2026: physically isolated EU infrastructure, a separate partition, EU-resident operations, and independent European governance. SCHUFA, ITZBund, and SAP are already on it — but what actually changes for those of us who build? In this session I'll cut through the marketing. We'll look at how the new partition differs from standard AWS (IAM, billing, Route 53, ARN formats), which serverless services are available at launch, and the concrete steps to migrate a CDK stack across. I'll give an honest assessment of the CLOUD Act question — what sovereignty does and doesn't protect against — and a decision framework for when the Sovereign Cloud is genuinely required versus when a normal EU region is enough. We'll ground it in the regulation driving these projects across DACH: NIS2, DORA, GDPR, and the EU AI Act. Expect a practical, vendor-fluff-free walkthrough with a live CDK migration, aimed at architects and developers running compliance-sensitive workloads in Germany, Austria, and Switzerland.
